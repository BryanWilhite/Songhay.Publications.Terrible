Currently and sadly most of the digital media products of the Songhay System were produced in the late-20<sup>th</sup> and early-21<sup>st</sup> centuries 😐📆 These productions include:

- information design, visual design and publication-automation of one of the oldest “ethnic” arts projects on the Internet, [kintespace.com](https://kintespace.com/), standing on the shoulders of activist artists like [Michael Massenburg](https://inglewoodopenstudios.org/michaelmassenburg), [Lisa Diane Wedgeworth](https://www.lisadianewedgeworth.com/) and [MonaLisa Whitaker](https://www.monalisawhitaker.com/about-me)
- award-winning sound design for the one-woman show _Shak’n_ by Julliard alumnae [Saundra Quarterman](https://www.imdb.com/name/nm0702944/)
- motion graphics for the HBO-aired documentary, _Beah: A Black Woman Speaks_, directed by [LisaGay Hamilton](https://www.imdb.com/name/nm0004986/?ref_=tt_ov_1_1) and edited by Oscar-winner, [Kate Amend](https://www.imdb.com/name/nm0024569/?ref_=fn_t_1)
- producer for _William Pajaud: Art & Work_, directed by [Blaze Hall](https://www.imdb.com/name/nm4302159/?ref_=ttfc_fcr_1_1)
